# DuckDash

## Pizza worth waddling for.

Fresh ideas. Hot pizza. Happy ducks.

**Your order. Your number. Your duck.**

Order at the front desk, collect your numbered duck, and watch the board. When your number is **Ready**, your imaginary pizza is ready to collect.

## Specials and coupons

**Student special — 20% off**

Show your student card at the desk. One eligible student discount per order.

**DUCK10 — 10% off**

Present coupon code **DUCK10** at the desk.

**FLOCK5 — £5 off orders of £20 or more**

Present coupon code **FLOCK5**. The basket subtotal must be at least £20 before any discount.

**The best eligible single discount wins. Discounts do not stack.** The basket subtotal includes pizza prices, large upgrades and extra toppings. If several discounts qualify, DuckDash applies the one that saves the most. Prices and discounts are calculated using whole pence; percentage discounts are rounded to the nearest penny, with half pennies rounded up.

## Collection board

**Preparing** — your order is in the kitchen.

**Ready** — collect at the front desk using the number on your duck.

## Menu footer

DuckDash is a fictional takeaway for a teaching activity. All money, food and preparation times are simulated. No payment is taken. Ingredient descriptions are part of the exercise and are not dietary or allergen advice.

## Suggested gradual menu reveals

1. **First orders:** the Classic Quack and Pepperoni Paddle, regular size, no offers.
2. **Make it yours:** reveal the full menu, large pizzas and extra toppings.
3. **Student special:** introduce student eligibility and the 20% rule.
4. **Coupon chaos:** introduce DUCK10 and FLOCK5, then compare eligible discounts.
5. **The lunch rush:** accept several pizzas in one order and follow different order numbers on the collection board.
