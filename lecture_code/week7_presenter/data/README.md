# Bristol data used in Week 7

Downloaded 31 October 2026. These are real observations, not simulated teaching data. The duck image and small A/B/C tables are explicitly designed examples.

## Traffic

Source: Department for Transport road traffic API, https://roadtraffic.dft.gov.uk/api/raw-counts . Query: filter[local_authority_id]=144, filter[year]=2023, page[size]=10000. The current authority list identifies 144 as Bristol, City of (E06000023); do not copy the unrelated identifier 147 from older search snippets.

Original response: raw/traffic-2023.json. All 936 records were returned in one page (last_page=1), representing 40 count points and 33 count dates. One record is one site/date/hour/direction; hours are 7 through 18 inclusive. These are sampled raw surveys, not annual average daily flow or continuous monitoring. A site may have one or two recorded directions.

bristol_traffic_2023.csv retains the count-point key, date, hour and direction, and renames pedal_cycles→bicycles, cars_and_taxis→cars, buses_and_coaches→buses, lgvs→vans, all_hgvs→lorries, all_motor_vehicles→motor_vehicles. Cars therefore includes taxis; buses includes coaches. Motor vehicles includes categories not individually shown, including two-wheeled motor vehicles. Do not add motor_vehicles to its constituent categories.

count_points.csv contains distinct site metadata from the same response: road name, coordinates, and junction names. The merge key is unique. Coordinates are longitude/latitude in degrees. Road name alone is not unique (multiple points can lie on one road).

For comparing locations, code first sums directions within site/date/hour, then averages these site-hours per site. This avoids weighting two-direction sites twice in an across-site mean. It does not remove differences in survey dates, season or sampling design. Map circles encode mean observed hourly motor counts, not annual daily estimates. One selected A38 site (6401) was counted on 13 July 2023.

DfT guidance and downloads: https://roadtraffic.dft.gov.uk/downloads . Public-sector information under the Open Government Licence v3.0: https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/ . Retain attribution and the source's data limitations.

## Air quality

Bristol City Council: Air quality monitors with annual mean NO2 measurements.
Catalogue: https://www.arcgis.com/home/item.html?id=925cadfd3a034df3beea8f0088a942d0
Service: https://maps2.bristol.gov.uk/server2/rest/services/ext/ll_environment_and_planning/MapServer/8

Original GeoJSON: raw/air-quality-2023.geojson. Query selects UGM3_2023 IS NOT NULL; fields SITEID, LOCATION, UGM3_2023, COUNT_2023; coordinates requested as EPSG:4326. All 196 returned features are retained in bristol_no2_2023.csv, with year=2023. no2_ugm3 is annual mean nitrogen dioxide in micrograms per cubic metre; sample_count is the source COUNT_2023 field. It is retained so incomplete sampling is visible, not interpreted as an hourly reading count. Do not assume equal sampling completeness across sites.

This layer contains annual monitoring-site means, not the continuous hourly dataset discussed during planning. It is suitable for an explicitly annual map overlay. No interpolation, nearest-site pairing or causal model is applied. Sampled traffic days and annual pollution means are not aligned observations. The map is exploratory and does not establish effects of the Clean Air Zone.

## Map background

Bristol City Council classified streets:
https://maps2.bristol.gov.uk/server2/rest/services/ext/ll_transport/MapServer/7

raw/bristol-classified-streets.geojson contains 112 street features; raw/roads-metadata.json preserves the layer metadata. bristol_roads.json stores their line coordinates for lightweight plotting. This is a classified-street backdrop, not every street in Bristol. It is a current background download, not a reconstruction of the 2023 street network. The source layer's copyrightText is empty; retain council attribution and consult the publisher before redistributing the geometry outside the course. No OpenStreetMap data are bundled: the attempted OSM service downloads failed.

## Derived activity data

activities.json contains the same traffic summaries, air measurements and street lines for browser diagrams. It is generated by prepare_data.py; do not edit it independently. Missing values are not converted to zero. The map uses a local longitude/latitude aspect adjustment; this is a teaching visual rather than a survey-grade GIS analysis.
